Redirect Cleaner & Categorizer

Upload the URL redirects XLSX export.
Expected columns: rec_id | src_url | tgt_url | shop_code | status_code

What this tool does:
1. Removes rows where the source URL belongs to a non-.com domain (.nl, .de, .fr, .it, .es)
2. Resolves redirect chains (A→B, B→C becomes A→C)
3. Categorizes remaining redirects into tabs: type_1, type_2, type_3, type_4, webshop, errors
4. Outputs a clean XLSX with 6 tabs
