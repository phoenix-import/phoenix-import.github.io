Load existing products from Shopify

Step 1. Enter the SKUs to load, one per line or separated by commas, and generate the read script. Paste it into the storefront DevTools console (manibhadra-phoeniximport.myshopify.com) and run it. It changes nothing in Shopify; it downloads a phoenix-spec-load-….json file. To copy the result instead, type copy(specLoad) in the console when it is done.
Step 2. Paste the result here, or pick the downloaded file. Each SKU becomes a card with its current specs filled in. Change what you need and generate the write script as usual: it overwrites the values, and any field you emptied on a loaded product is deleted from Shopify.
