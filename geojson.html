Draw on Map

How to draw a plot:
  • Click the polygon button (□) on the map
  • Click on the map to add corners
  • Click the first point again to finish
  • Click a shape to change it
  • Use trash icon to delete shapes
How to place a point:
  • Click the marker button on the map
  • Click the spot where the plot is
  • Fill in the plot area (ha) in the popup that opens
  • Hollow red points still need an area
  • Use the edit or trash icon to move or delete points
